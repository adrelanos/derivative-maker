DhampirOS by potemkin, 2026.

The illustrations were generated for this independent Vampire Hunter D
fan project. No source film frames are distributed in these assets.
Vector artwork, interface styling, motion and synthetic event sounds were
created for DhampirOS. This is an interactive visual study of a pre-alpha
Linux project, not a recording of a booted system.
